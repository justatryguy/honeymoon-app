BEFORE THE CURTAIN

Essentials

112
Police, ambulance and fire in all three countries — any phone, no SIM, English answered. Full emergency page behind the 112 button, top right, from anywhere in the app.
MONEY
Francs in Switzerland, euros in Austria, forints in Hungary. Cards near-universal; carry cash for mountain huts and small vendors.
WEATHER
Cities 54–72°F, cold above 2,000m (41–50°F). Layers, a light waterproof, and shoes that forgive cobblestones.
PLUGS
Switzerland runs its own Type J — a grounded Schuko will not fit, so bring a Swiss-specific adapter for anything chunky. Austria and Hungary take the ordinary European pin. All 230V; dual-voltage gear needs no converter.
BORDERS & PHONES
First Schengen entry (Zurich, Friday night) registers fingerprints and a photo — allow queue time. ETIAS is not in force; nothing to apply for. Passports must be valid three months past Oct 4. Load an eSIM that explicitly covers Switzerland and the EU — many plans exclude the Swiss legs.
